Hi, I'm Tamryn Ripepi.

My Story

I was born and raised in Mitchells Plain, Cape Town. My journey into IT started when I attended Westcliff School of Skills in Bellville. While I was there, I took part in an Office Administration workshop where I learned typing skills and how to work with computers. This is where I started becoming interested in computers and realised that I wanted to work in the IT industry one day.

After high school, I applied to colleges such as CPUT, Northlink College and False Bay College because I wanted to study IT. Unfortunately, I couldn't get accepted because I didn't have my Grade 12. The school I attended was a School of Skills and did not offer Grade 12.

I then found Balco College in Mitchells Plain, where I could complete my matric. I couldn't go straight into Grade 12, so I had to start with Grade 9 first. After passing Grade 9, I moved on to Grade 12.

While studying matric at Balco College, I was also studying IT Technical Support at AtVance Academy. My matric classes were on Saturdays and my IT classes were on Tuesdays. The IT course was supposed to take one year, but because of COVID-19, it took me two years to complete. I passed my IT Technical Support NQF Level 4 course with 163 credits.

Unfortunately, Grade 12 was difficult for me, especially because COVID-19 came during that time. I struggled with some of my subjects and tried to get extra help from my teachers. After writing my final exams, I unfortunately failed matric. It was a very disappointing moment for me.

I didn't want to give up, so I decided to try again and rewrite the subjects I had failed. I didn't want to return to Balco College, so I applied to Abbotts College in Rondebosch as a part-time student. I decided to do the whole year again and work towards getting my matric. After writing my final exams, I finally passed! My marks weren't the best, but I was very happy and proud of myself because I knew how much I had gone through to get there.

After completing my studies in IT Technical Support, I still had an interest in technology, but I also wanted to learn how to code and build websites. I decided to take another step and enrolled at Life Choices Academy in Lansdowne, Cape Town, to study Software Development.

At Life Choices Academy, I learned about Frontend, Backend and Databases. I enjoyed building websites, working on projects and learning new things. I eventually graduated from the academy and continued working on improving my development skills.

Today, I'm still learning, improving my skills and exploring new technologies. I'm proud of how far I've come, and most importantly, I'm proud of myself for never giving up.
